Privacy and support

Calavera AR

Calavera AR paints a calavera on your face with the front camera. You can roll a new one, save a picture, or record a video.

What stays on your phone

  • The camera is used to draw the mask. The picture is processed on the device.
  • The microphone is used only while you record a video, so the clip can include your voice.
  • A picture or video is saved to your photo library only when you tap save.
  • There is no account. Nothing is uploaded to us. We do not track you.

Screen share

If you share your screen during a call in another app, that app sends whatever is on the screen. Calavera AR does not run the call.

Privacidad

Calavera AR pinta una calavera en tu cara con la cámara frontal. La imagen se procesa en el teléfono. El micrófono se usa solo al grabar un video. Una foto o un video se guarda en tu fototeca solo cuando tú lo pides. No hay cuenta y no subimos nada.
